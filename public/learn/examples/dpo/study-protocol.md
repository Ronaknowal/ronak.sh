## Questions and interpretation

The constructed study asks whether a complete causal model update changes the reference-relative likelihood of supplied comparisons, and whether that change also improves an independently specified grammar/request criterion on free generations. The real-data study asks how a deliberately small, randomly initialized model and current DPOTrainer process released human comparisons and behave on reserved prompt families. It demonstrates the objective and API; it is not a useful assistant or a new human-preference benchmark.

No result is promised. Retain failed comparisons, malformed tokens, early EOS, horizon endings, unfavorable baseline comparisons and all declared checkpoints. No fit is repeated to improve a narrative. A scientific source change requires a new explicit continuation and output directory; layout, wording and source-report repairs reuse unchanged fitted observations.

## A. Exact algebra, masks and cache

These are deterministic mechanism checks, not empirical fits. Use float64 for the standard-library algebra and PyTorch finite-difference/autograd comparison where possible. Verify four-score margin/loss/partials; stable logits of magnitude 1000; exponential tilt and exact objective identity; shared reward shift and reward/beta co-scaling; invalid support; same-prompt normalizer cancellation; two different prompts with uncancelled constants; constrained three-output parameter sharing; repeated-label logistic optimum; conservative-label objective; IPO target; summed versus mean log-ratio comparison.

Use right-padded mixed-length token rows with distinct attention and completion masks. Score the first response from the immediately preceding logit, include genuine EOS and exclude padding by position. Check single/batched agreement, added-padding invariance, PAD=EOS, no response, whole-pair context overflow and a verified tokenization-boundary merge example. A no-target pair is an explicit validation error, not an arbitrary zero training loss.

For caching, compute exact frozen-reference response sums, store semantic input identities and pair order, and compare cached/uncached policy loss and gradients. An updated policy retains a valid cache; altered reference weights, tokenizer/template, token IDs, completion mask or pair ordering fails validation. No stochastic dropout or implicit reference synchronization is allowed.

## B. Constructed causal-model study

Vocabulary in order: PAD, BOS, EOS, one, sentence, France, Italy, Paris, Rome, is, capital, of, period. The exact token IDs are 0 through 12. Four prompts are BOS followed by an instruction (one or sentence) and a country (France or Italy), in instruction-major then country order. Short responses are the corresponding capital plus EOS. Long responses are capital, is, capital, of, country, period, EOS. There are eight demonstrations: both short and long responses for every prompt. There are eight constructed comparisons: the instruction-appropriate response beats the alternative length, and beats the same response with the other country's capital substituted as its first token. No human annotation is claimed.

Use the exact inspected causal prerequisite classes (token/position embeddings, two pre-normalized causal blocks and vocabulary head), with vocabulary 13, width 32, two heads, two layers and context 16. No dropout. CPU float32, two Torch threads, seed 41 for initialization. The wrapper adds no reward or value head.

Response-only SFT runs exactly 120 full-batch AdamW updates, learning rate .003, weight decay 0 and gradient-norm limit 1. The objective is mean negative log probability over valid response targets across the eight demonstrations. Record steps 0, 30, 60, 90 and 120. Freeze a deep copy of the resulting policy as reference, with gradients disabled and evaluation mode. This is a new DPO experiment's initialization, not a replay or continuation of the preceding RLHF fit.

From that one checkpoint, retain an unchanged baseline and make two independent copies. DPO uses all eight pairs in each of 40 updates, beta .2, standard sigmoid objective, summed response log probabilities, mean over pairs, AdamW learning rate .001, weight decay 0 and gradient-norm limit 1. The chosen-only continuation uses the eight chosen responses for 40 updates under response-token mean NLL and the same optimizer settings. These are matched update counts, not matched compute or token budgets; report actual response-target counts and forward/backward roles instead of claiming a fair performance contest. Both copies start with fresh optimizer moments.

Record pair diagnostics and free generations at updates 0, 10, 20, 30 and 40. For each checkpoint, generate eight responses for each of the four prompts, temperature 1, full vocabulary, generator seed 941 reset for each checkpoint/method, at most eight newly generated actions, stop at EOS. A PAD or BOS draw is a real action and a malformed output, not silently masked away. Retain all 32 literal token sequences per checkpoint with IDs, stop cause, length and criterion. The criterion is exact equality to the instruction-appropriate constructed response; report that narrow criterion separately from likelihood margins and from grammar/EOS counts. The fixed prompts are training prompts, so this is a controlled mechanism study, not held-out generalization.

Record exact reference weight identity before and after every method; it must remain unchanged. Save enough first-minibatch values to independently reconstruct all eight chosen/rejected sequence scores, relative margins, per-pair losses, mean loss and update counts. No beta, seed or learning-rate sweep is planned.
