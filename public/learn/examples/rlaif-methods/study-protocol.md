| Arm | Objective and coefficient | Declared convention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SFT | Mean of per-response chosen-token NLL | Response-only, then equal pair weighting. |
| DPO | Mean softplus(−.2h) | h from summed completion policy-reference gains. |
