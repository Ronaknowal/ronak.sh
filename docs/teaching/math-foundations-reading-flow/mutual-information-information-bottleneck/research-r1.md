## Canonical coverage audit and deliberate placement

S1 chapter2 contains entropy, joint/conditional entropy, relative entropy/MI, their identities, chain rules, inequalities, DPI, the statistical second-law discussion, sufficiency and Fano. Entropy owns the underlying entropy/KL mechanics; this lesson reconstructs only the joint/conditional operation needed to understand dependence. Chain rules, DPI and sufficiency are core. Fano was missing from the old lesson's explicit teaching and earns a small deeper error-partition treatment because it links information to a concrete prediction decision without equating MI with accuracy. Its full finite mechanism and code are planned, not name-dropped. The thermodynamic/statistical second-law material requires a different stochastic-process question and is omitted with its assumptions intact.

S2 adds measure-general MI and channel calculus. General KL meaning, singular limits and Gaussian examples are retained. Strong-DPI coefficients, tensorization proofs and capacity optimization need separate outcomes and are outside the title's present implementation promise. No dimension-count shortcut is substituted for conditional-channel structure.

S3's central predictive distortion and all alternating minimizations remain visible. Complete original code and prior independent nonuniform auxiliary checks are stronger conservation requirements than preserving a headline equation. Its deterministic annealing/phase-transition discussion becomes an annotated advanced route, not a claim that sweeping beta with one initialization finds a unique best frontier. The finite codeword alphabet is always declared.

S4 motivates one new bounded computational example. Actual VI prior code teaches stochastic Gaussian gradients; it does not already implement a supervised decoder. Thus C12 must provide complete local hand-gradient and ordinary Torch comparison, objective/reduction/units and one update. The learning outcome is computing and debugging that bound, not a promised end-to-end image-classifier trainer. A changed-noise row and lambda are independent transfer tasks.

S5–S8 strengthen the existing advanced-estimation bridge. The current Contrastive destination is still unpublished; its preserved note precisely owns a full finite enumeration and eventual trainer. Local figures can illustrate candidate generation and distinguish the expected objective from one score without duplicating an unrequested trainer. MINE is an optional source for why a critic-based statistic is not automatically a finite-data bound. Do not assert that every neural estimator has a hard logN ceiling; exact known distributions and structured assumptions lie outside the quoted distribution-free setting.
