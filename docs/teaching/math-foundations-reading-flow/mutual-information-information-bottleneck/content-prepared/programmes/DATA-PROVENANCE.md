# Offline data for the information example

`penguins.csv` is the Palmer penguins simplified dataset distributed under CC0 by the palmerpenguins project. It is reused byte-for-byte from the preceding Entropy lesson's retained research input; no new download or new collection is claimed here.

SHA256: `f204db2c753b0937caac3cb35258562c14f073e4bbc76be24b4c51ce22767a93`.

[Dataset documentation](https://allisonhorst.github.io/palmerpenguins/) describes the observations and distribution. [Gorman, Williams and Fraser (2014)](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0090081) describes the underlying field study. Keep this attribution with the offline file.

This lesson uses species, island and year, present in all 344 records. Missing body measurements do not cause row deletion. The computed information describes the empirical study table and a specified grouping of island categories; it is not a population-census estimate or a causal effect.
