## Scope and research decisions

Canonical treatment one is the complete author-hosted ISLP Chapter5 Python resampling lab; treatment two is the official scikit-learn CV/search teaching sequence plus APIs. These complement the retained ESL Chapter7 section map; full ESL chapter access failed and is not claimed. Original research supplies selection bias, variance limits, random search, TPE and Hyperband. Bates–Hastie–Tibshirani supplies the new precise estimand and holdout-MSE distinction under explicit assumptions. This is sufficient depth to author; paper experiments are not a licence to promise universal method rankings.

Include holdout, K-fold/stratified/repeated/Monte Carlo/LOO/Leave-P variants at mechanism-appropriate depth, group/time/custom restrictions, leakage and inner ownership, OOF coverage, selection bias/nesting/refit, conditional/random search, uncertainty/bootstrap, adaptive/multi-fidelity search, failures/budgeting and reporting. Keep threshold selection and early stopping as information-boundary applications; their optimizing algorithms belong to model-specific/DL owners. Keep full permutation inference, selective inference, conformal prediction, spatial causal transport, comprehensive Bayesian-optimization GP kernels, BOHB/ASHA/distributed schedulers and multiple-testing corrections as named later study with concrete reasons: they require new estimands or algorithms beyond this lesson's core procedure. No claims that ordinary iid permutation is valid for dependent groups/time. Regression analytic LOO/regularization paths are a bridge to Regularization; no duplicate solver programme.
