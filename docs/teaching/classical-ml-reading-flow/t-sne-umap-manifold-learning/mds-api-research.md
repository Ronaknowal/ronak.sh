# Research and coverage decisions

As of 8 October 2026. Scope: mechanisms, implementation practice and interpretation for Classical ML17; foundational sources through current maintained documentation, with a targeted later-literature check around sampled objectives, attraction/repulsion and density. This is not an exhaustive 2026 publication census, a biomedical interpretation or a reproduction study. Dates below distinguish original publication from documentation accessed now. All proposed scientific outputs remain unexecuted; old results keep their original attribution.

## Canonical treatments actually consulted

1. **van der Maaten, Postma & van den Herik (2009), [Dimensionality Reduction: A Comparative Review](https://lvdmaaten.github.io/publications/papers/TR_Dimensionality_Reduction_Review_2009.pdf).** Read introduction/taxonomy and selected method treatments §§3.1.1–3.1.5 and §§3.2.1–3.2.3 in the 36-page text. It supports contrasting distance, local reconstruction and neighborhood objectives rather than calling every map a nonlinear PCA. Whole benchmark tables and all later methods were not read. Design use: MDS/Isomap/LLE/graph comparison and their assumptions. Preserve locally derived fixtures; do not inherit the survey's entire taxonomy as universal optimization guarantees.
2. **[scikit-learn Manifold Learning chapter 2.2](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/manifold.html), actual accessed header 1.9.1.** Read the method descriptions, classical/metric/nonmetric MDS distinction, LLE variants, spectral embedding, t-SNE and practical notes. This is the second broad canonical treatment and the ordinary-tool comparison. Costs depend on neighbor search, matrix representation and solver; the chapter's illustrative expressions are not universal bounds for every implementation. Design use: complete small ordinary routes and API distinctions, with explicit scratch conventions and current-version source checks before execution.
3. **Saul & Roweis (2001 draft), [An Introduction to Locally Linear Embedding](https://cs.nyu.edu/home/people/in_memoriam/roweis/lle/papers/lleintro.pdf).** Read introduction, algorithm explanation, selected §3 and Appendices A/B; inspected Figure 1/2 captions. The PDF text has garbled mathematical glyphs. It supports the reconstruction-then-embedding ladder, regularization and constrained eigenproblem; exact displayed equations will be checked against official implementation and our derivation. The original Science PDF link failed, so this is an accessed creator treatment, not a false claim of reading that original PDF.

Author-hosted *Elements of Statistical Learning* landing page was accessible, but the linked/attempted book PDF was not. Bishop's large PDF also failed access. Neither inaccessible book is counted as read or as one of the two required treatments.

## Official tools and interpretation resources

* [TSNE API](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.manifold.TSNE.html), accessed 1.9.1, selected parameter/default descriptions. Explain `init='pca'`, perplexity < n, `max_iter`, method/angle and learning-rate conventions. The API specifies `auto=max(n/(early_exaggeration*4),50)`; do not copy another implementation's learning-rate number unchanged. Keep old explicit arguments and version attribution; no migration rerun is needed for saved results.
* [ClassicalMDS API](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.manifold.ClassicalMDS.html) and [MDS API](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.manifold.MDS.html), accessed 1.9.1: actual constructor, stress/initialization parameters and input/fit sections read. `ClassicalMDS(metric='precomputed')` is the separate eigendecomposition route. Iterative `metric_mds` chooses metric/nonmetric stress; `metric='precomputed'` supplies a square dissimilarity matrix. Explicit starts/restart count/seed/stress avoid changing defaults. Nonmetric zero input dissimilarities mean missing, unlike classical known duplicate distances. Current installed constructor/array-start/stress source was inspected without execution; [R1 amendment](design-api-correction/mds-contract-amendment.md) binds exact decisions and limits. Deprecated aliases still work in1.9; no old failure, fit or numerical parity is inferred.
* [UMAP how it works](https://umap-learn.readthedocs.io/en/latest/how_umap_works.html), selected graph explanation; [reproducibility](https://umap-learn.readthedocs.io/en/latest/reproducibility.html) and [transform](https://umap-learn.readthedocs.io/en/latest/transform.html), page identity/selected introductions. Headers identify 0.5.8 documentation, whereas retained runs are 0.5.12. Use these to frame source-version checks, not to falsely certify a matched new execution. JIT startup, threads and randomness are distinct from stable scientific identity.
* [UMAP implementation source](https://umap-learn.readthedocs.io/en/latest/_modules/umap/umap_.html), actual `smooth_knn_dist` implementation, density-radius calculation and `transform` guard inspected. Self column is excluded from the calibration sum; nonzero distances and limited-support cases matter. Density radii use weighted squared graph distances and a log, not a calibrated global probability density. Bind exact installed source/version before an ordinary-library comparison in content phase.
* [densMAP tutorial](https://umap-learn.readthedocs.io/en/latest/densmap_demo.html), parameter examples and transformation section inspected. Explain density regularization as a tradeoff; do not borrow its timings or interpret cell biology here. Its example reuses training input, and source can return the cached embedding before checking densMAP. For genuinely new input, the inspected implementation raises `NotImplementedError`. Thus ordinary UMAP's out-of-sample route must not be extended to densMAP on the strength of that example.
* [openTSNE parameter guide](https://opentsne.readthedocs.io/en/stable/parameters.html), actual header 1.0.0 and full short guide read. Explain perplexity, exaggeration, clipping for new-point embedding and interpolation/tree controls. This family can support reference-map extension; scikit-learn TSNE does not supply `transform`. The guide is not a matched installed environment or an executed tutorial. Any full new source is explicitly source-only until its actual dependency/setup is verified.
* Wattenberg, Viégas & Johnson (2016), [How to Use t-SNE Effectively](https://distill.pub/2016/misread-tsne/), selected examples and interpretation discussion. Useful alternate explanation of cluster area, inter-island distance, convergence and perplexity. Its simulations are the authors' examples, not our saved optical-digit runs. Do not inherit a universal parameter recipe. Reference paper/media annotations distinguish text/captions read from pixels or video viewed.
