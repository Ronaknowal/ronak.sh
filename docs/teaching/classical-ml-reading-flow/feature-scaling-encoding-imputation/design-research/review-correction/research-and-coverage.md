## Misconceptions explicitly tested

Scaling is not making data Gaussian or making features equally predictive. Robust scaling is not outlier removal. Zero fitted variance does not force every later value to zero. Row normalization and column scaling preserve different information. Dropping one-hot columns is not universally mandatory. Missing, unknown and rare are different states. A fill is not measured truth. MAR is conditional and is not diagnosed from a mask alone. Imputation reconstruction quality and downstream task quality are different objectives. Query features can leak into fitting even without targets. Query-batch ranks are not a fitted quantile map. New basis coordinates change capacity. Arbitrary IDs are not ordered quantities. Smoothing alone does not exclude targets. OOF training codes differ from full-fit inference codes. Expanding time splits are not automatically valid TargetEncoder cv. Signs do not eliminate collisions. Deterministic iterative completion is not proper MI. Averaging tables is not pooling analyses. More imputations do not repair an inappropriate model or identify MNAR.
