# Tokenization: beginner reading revision

## User finding and scope

28 September 2026: the user found the newly completed three lessons harder to
understand than the preserved originals. Numerical correctness and working labs
did not establish an accessible first reading. This revision improves all three
reading paths, preserves their scratch/library depth, and restores meaningful
coloured token representations. The original rollback snapshots remain protected.

The previous integration/author/independent receipts are historical evidence for
their exact bytes. Do not change those receipts to certify this new prose. The
central ledger records revision 2 with the earlier entries preserved. Unchanged
model/program verification can be reused with exact hash comparisons; rewritten
prose, new diagrams and presentation require new review and browser observations.

## Gaps and teaching decisions

- Vocabulary, token IDs and embedding rows appeared before a concrete conversion
  of a familiar word into pieces and numbered dictionary entries.
- Terminology and pipeline caveats accumulated faster than the mechanism was
  explained. The older version supplied more narrative reasons and recognizable
  coloured pieces, although several of its scientific claims needed correction.
- Large optimized source files arrived before learners could trace their main
  operations. Introduce a small worked operation and readable core first, then
  map it to the unchanged efficient implementation and ordinary library route.
- Advanced sections also need local buildup: a complete segmentation before
  a probability lattice, fractional counts before EM, concrete text/bytes before
  architecture families, finite dictionary choices before allocation formulas.
- Use colour for stable piece identity or a stated representational role, with
  literal labels and boundaries retained. Keep page surfaces neutral and controls
  amber. Selection must remain distinguishable from token identity.
